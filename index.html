WHO WE ARE

More than a church.
We're a family.

GraceLife Church is a welcoming community learning to love God, love people, and live with purpose. Wherever you are on your faith journey, there is room for you here.

We believe in the transforming power of Jesus, the truth of Scripture, and the beauty of doing life together—with grace, compassion, and courage.
